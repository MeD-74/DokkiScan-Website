The benefits of X-rays in medicine

X-rays remain the first imaging test in most clinics because they are fast, affordable and available almost everywhere. A chest X-ray can be taken in minutes, a fracture can be confirmed on the spot, and a dentist can check a tooth before starting treatment.

They also guide follow-up: comparing a new image with an older one shows whether a fracture is healing or a chest infection is clearing. Newer tests are more detailed, but the plain X-ray still answers many everyday questions.
